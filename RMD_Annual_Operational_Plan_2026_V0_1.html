Executive Summary
Plan purpose, V0.1 scope and required decisions

Plan Purpose. The plan establishes a unified operating framework for safety, asset performance, projects, contracts, budget, resources, risks, and digital transformation. Version 0.1 uses available data and clearly identifies data gaps to be completed before Version 1.0.

هدف الخطة. تهدف الخطة إلى توحيد إطار العمل التشغيلي لمحاور السلامة، أداء الأصول، المشاريع، العقود، الميزانية، الموارد، المخاطر، والتحول الرقمي. يعتمد الإصدار 0.1 على البيانات المتوفرة حالياً، مع تحديد فجوات البيانات المطلوب استكمالها قبل إصدار النسخة 1.0.

What is Confirmed

  • Plan structure across 8 operational pillars
  • 30 projects portfolio · AED 406.37M total value
  • Approved 2026 budget envelope AED 121.92M
  • 10 executive KPI definitions and targets
  • Risk register with controls and ownership
  • Digital transformation initiatives and timeline

What is Pending

  • Operational KPI baseline values (Maximo extraction)
  • MTTR / MTBF by system and asset class
  • Validated contractor KPI scores
  • Actual spending and commitments alignment
  • Critical spares register and minimum stock levels
  • Failure taxonomy and reliability baseline

Version 0.1 Scope

  • Internal working baseline only — not for external publication
  • Single source for Director and Executive review
  • Transition framework towards Asset Performance Plan
  • Identifies governance, owners and data gaps
  • Foundation for V1.0 with validated operational data

Key Decisions Required

  • Endorse V0.1 as the internal working baseline
  • Approve 10 KPI monthly executive dashboard
  • Assign data owners for V1.0 completion
  • Approve Asset Performance Plan transition
  • Establish Reliability and RCA Board (monthly)
  • Establish Digital Maintenance Steering Review (quarterly)

Transformation Roadmap
Click a stage to expand activities
1
Data Clean Up
Validate Maximo data, define failure taxonomy
Activities: Failure record audit · Asset hierarchy alignment · Cleansing of duplicate work orders · Data owner assignment.
Outcome: Trusted baseline data for analytics.
2
Asset Criticality
Classify assets by service and safety impact
Activities: Criticality matrix · Safety / availability / cost weighting · Single criticality register per system.
Outcome: Prioritised maintenance focus.
3
Reliability Baseline
Establish MTBF, MTTR and failure mode baseline
Activities: System-level MTBF/MTTR · Top failure modes per asset class · Pareto for service affecting failures.
Outcome: Quantified reliability starting point.
4
Predictive Maintenance
Move from time based to condition and AI based
Activities: AI predictive models · Condition monitoring · Wayside sensors · Turnout monitoring trial.
Outcome: Reduced unplanned failures and intervention waste.
5
Asset Performance Governance
Embed monthly performance and reliability boards
Activities: Reliability & RCA Board · Digital Maintenance Steering · KPI review cycle · Continuous improvement.
Outcome: Sustained outcome-based culture.
